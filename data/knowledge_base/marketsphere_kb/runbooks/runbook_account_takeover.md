# Suspected Account Takeover

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Red Flags

- Customer contacts saying they didn't authorize recent orders
- Login from unusual location followed by address change
- Multiple failed login attempts followed by successful login
- Order shipped to unfamiliar address followed by customer dispute
- Email associated with account suddenly changed

## Immediate Actions

1. Lock the account (Account > Admin > Lock).
2. Cancel any in-flight orders that haven't shipped.
3. For shipped orders, attempt carrier intercept if possible.
4. Reset password through verified email.
5. Notify customer through known-good channel (alternate email or phone if available).

## Verification

Before unlocking:
- Verify customer through multiple factors (see Identity Verification runbook).
- Have customer change password.
- Review and confirm shipping addresses on file.
- Review and confirm payment methods on file (remove any added during takeover window).

## Aftermath

- Refund any unauthorized charges promptly.
- File fraud report internally.
- Submit fraud reports to card networks if cards were used.
- Document the incident timeline.
