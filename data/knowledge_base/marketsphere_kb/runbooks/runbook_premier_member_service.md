# Premier Member (Platinum Tier) Service Standards

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Premier Member Identification

Platinum-tier members are marked with a gold badge in the CRM. Their accounts have spent $5000+ in the rolling 12 months.

## Service Standards

- Skip the queue: route directly to senior agent
- 24/7 priority phone line: 1-800-555-PREM
- Dedicated email: premier@marketsphere.example
- First-contact resolution rate target: 90%

## Authority

For Premier members, agents have expanded authority:
- Tier 1 refund authority: $500 (up from $200)
- Goodwill credits: $100 (up from $25)
- Concierge support: order placement, gift selection, special requests

## Goodwill Standard

Premier members generally receive the benefit of the doubt in disputes. For first-time issues, default to the customer's preferred resolution.

## Important

Premier service does not mean different policies. It means same policies executed with priority and flexibility. Do not violate fraud, age verification, or safety procedures regardless of tier.
