# Refund to Alternate Payment Method

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Default Rule

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. This is the law in many jurisdictions and our standard policy.

## Exception Cases

Sometimes the original method is unavailable:
- Card has been closed
- Card has expired
- Account closed (PayPal etc.)
- Customer is no longer at the issuing bank

## Process for Alternate Refund

1. Verify identity at Level 2.
2. Document the reason the original method is unavailable.
3. Options, in order of preference:
   a. Send to a new card the customer provides (must be issued to same person)
   b. Issue as store credit (preferred for low-value)
   c. Mail a check (for refunds > $500 where the customer has no other option)

## Never

- Refund to an account or card that is not the customer's own.
- Process refund to a "friend's" card.
- Accept third-party requests to redirect refunds.

These rules exist to prevent fraud and money-laundering. There is no flexibility on this.
