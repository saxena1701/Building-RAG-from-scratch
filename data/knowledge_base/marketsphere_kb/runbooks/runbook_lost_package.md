# Lost Package Investigation Procedure

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Definition

A package is considered "lost" when:
- Tracking shows "delivered" but customer reports non-receipt 48+ hours later, OR
- Tracking shows no movement for 5+ business days after carrier acceptance, OR
- Carrier confirms loss in their internal investigation.

## Customer-Facing Steps

1. Ask customer to:
   - Check around delivery location, with neighbors, building staff.
   - Check carrier's "left at" notes for unusual delivery spots.
   - Wait 24-48 hours after tracking shows delivered (occasional pre-scan).

2. If still missing, open carrier trace:
   - UPS: form UPS-110 in carrier portal
   - FedEx: trace request through API integration
   - USPS: file missing mail search at usps.com/help

3. Provide customer with case number and 5-business-day timeline.

## Resolution Path

After carrier investigation (or 5 days, whichever first):
- **If carrier confirms lost**: reship at no charge (expedited) OR full refund per customer preference.
- **If carrier marks delivered with proof**: review with senior agent. Considerations: customer history, value, signature/photo evidence. Often replace if account is in good standing and value < $200.
- **If customer has Package Protection**: reship immediately without waiting for carrier resolution.

## Anti-Fraud Considerations

Watch for patterns: same customer, multiple lost-package claims in 90 days, high-value items, address mismatches. Flag and escalate to fraud team if pattern detected.
