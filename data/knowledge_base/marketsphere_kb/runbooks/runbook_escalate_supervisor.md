# Escalation to Supervisor

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## When to Escalate

- Customer explicitly requests a supervisor (always honor)
- Resolution requires exceeding your refund/credit authority
- Legal mentions (lawsuit, attorney, regulator, BBB, news media)
- Threats or abusive behavior toward agents
- Issues affecting multiple customers (potential incident)
- High-profile customer accounts (flagged in CRM)

## Authority Limits

- Tier 1: Refunds up to $200, credits up to $25
- Tier 2: Refunds up to $1000, credits up to $100
- Tier 3 / Supervisor: Refunds up to $5000, credits up to $500
- Director-level: Unlimited

## Pre-Escalation Steps

Before transferring, capture:
1. Summary of the issue in CRM (2-3 sentences).
2. Resolution attempts you've made.
3. What the customer is asking for.
4. Any sensitive context (mental health concerns, accessibility needs, etc.).

## During Transfer

- Tell the customer you're connecting them with someone who has more authority.
- Stay on the line until the supervisor picks up and briefly hands off; don't drop the customer back into queue.

## After Escalation

- Note the outcome in CRM if you receive feedback.
- Flag yourself for coaching if you escalated something that turned out to be within your authority - this is learning, not failure.
