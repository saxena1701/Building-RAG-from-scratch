# B2B Account Support

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Recognizing B2B Accounts

B2B accounts have:
- Tax-exempt certificate on file
- Net-30 payment terms (some accounts)
- Assigned account manager (for accounts > $50k/year)
- Volume pricing tier

## Service Differences

- Address account by company name, not individual contact
- Support inquiries often come from multiple people at the same company; verify each requestor's role
- Some operational requests (invoice copies, statement reconciliation) are normal

## Common Issues

- **Invoice/receipt requests**: Generate from billing portal, send to AR contact.
- **Tax exemption not applied**: Check certificate validity and state coverage. Refund tax if exemption was valid at purchase.
- **PO number reference**: Add PO to order; some companies require this for AP processing.
- **Multi-user account access**: Use B2B admin tools to add users; do not share credentials.

## Escalation

For accounts with assigned account managers, escalate complex issues directly to the account manager rather than working through standard support.
