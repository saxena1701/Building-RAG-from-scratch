# Chargeback Response Procedure

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## What is a Chargeback

A chargeback occurs when a customer's bank reverses a charge, typically initiated through the cardholder filing a dispute. MarketSphere receives notification from our payment processor and has a set window (usually 7-10 days) to respond.

## Initial Triage

When a chargeback notification arrives:
1. Look up the disputed order.
2. Categorize the reason code (fraud, item not received, item not as described, duplicate, etc.).
3. Gather evidence relevant to the reason code.

## Evidence to Gather

- Order details, including IP and device fingerprint at order time
- Shipping carrier proof of delivery
- Tracking history
- Any customer communications (emails, chats)
- Return policy and terms acceptance timestamp

## Customer Outreach

Before responding to the chargeback:
1. Contact the customer to understand their concern.
2. If it's a legitimate issue that can be resolved (refund, replacement), do so AND request the customer withdraw the chargeback.
3. If unable to reach the customer, proceed with response based on available evidence.

## Response Decision

**Fight the chargeback** when:
- Evidence clearly supports our position
- Order was legitimately fulfilled

**Accept the chargeback** when:
- We cannot prove delivery
- Customer issue is legitimate and we'd refund anyway

Accept and resolve customer-first. Never fight a chargeback solely on principle if the underlying issue is real.
