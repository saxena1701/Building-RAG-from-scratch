# Review Moderation Procedure

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Reviews Are Moderated For

- Profanity, slurs, harassment of other users or staff
- Personal information (names, addresses, contact info)
- Off-topic content (politics, religion unrelated to product)
- Content about competitors
- Fake content (paid reviews, seller self-reviews, AI-generated spam patterns)
- Specific claims about defects we cannot verify

## What Stays

- Negative opinions about products (we don't moderate based on sentiment)
- Comparisons to similar products on our site
- Constructive criticism, including detailed complaints
- Personal stories using the product

## Process

1. Review queue is processed within 24 hours.
2. Edge cases escalate to senior reviewer.
3. Customer is notified of rejected reviews with a reason.
4. Customer can edit and resubmit.

## Important Constraint

We do NOT remove reviews simply because they are negative or because sellers complain. Reviews are removed only for policy violations. Maintaining authentic reviews is essential to customer trust.
