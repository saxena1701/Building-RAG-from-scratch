# Product Recall Response

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## When We Issue a Recall

- Safety issue identified (us, manufacturer, CPSC, FDA, etc.)
- Quality issue serious enough to warrant proactive customer outreach
- Regulatory requirement

## Customer Outreach

1. Identify all customers who purchased the recalled item.
2. Send recall notice email (template R-001) with:
   - Description of the issue
   - Safety implications
   - Instructions (return, dispose, repair)
   - Compensation (full refund + return shipping)
3. Post on our Recalls page.
4. Add a banner to product page.
5. Pause new orders of the item.

## Customer Service Handling

When recalled-item customers contact us:
- Acknowledge the recall.
- Process return/refund without standard return policy restrictions.
- For safety recalls, do not ask customer to ship back if shipping the item is itself unsafe (e.g., lithium battery fire risk) - dispose instructions instead.
- Refund includes shipping, taxes, and any restocking-type fees waived.

## Escalation

Major recalls (safety, multiple injuries) involve external communications and legal. Do not improvise; escalate to incident commander.
