# High-Value Order Verification

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Definition

Orders over $2000 trigger additional verification, even on established accounts.

## Verification Steps

1. Confirm shipping address matches billing OR has prior order history.
2. Review payment method - is it new or established?
3. Check account history - any red flags?
4. For new accounts or new payment methods, request additional verification (callback to phone on file, document upload).

## Hold Reasons

If any factor is suspicious:
- Place order on hold (does not cancel).
- Send verification email with secure link.
- Wait 24 hours for customer response.

## Release

Once verified, release with note in order log. Customer is informed of the delay and apologized to.

## Cancel

If verification fails or customer doesn't respond:
- Cancel order after 48 hours.
- Refund pending authorization.
- Notify customer of cancellation reason.
