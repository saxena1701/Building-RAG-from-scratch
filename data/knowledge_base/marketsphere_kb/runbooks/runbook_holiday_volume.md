# Holiday Season Operations

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Volume Expectations

Black Friday through end of December typically sees 4-5x normal volume. Expect:
- Longer support response times
- Carrier delays
- Inventory fluctuations
- More escalations from delivery issues

## Pre-Season Prep (October-November)

- Review high-volume runbooks
- Ensure backup contact methods work
- Update holiday cutoff dates in templates

## During Season

- Be proactive: customers asking about delivery dates near cutoffs should be told plainly whether their order will arrive in time.
- Use the "Holiday Delay" template (H-401) when carrier delays are widespread.
- For gifts that will be late, offer: expedited shipping upgrade on next order, small goodwill credit, gift receipt explanation for the recipient.

## Common Holiday Issues

- Gift recipients getting in touch about returns: handle as gift returns per standard runbook.
- "I didn't get my package, I needed it for Christmas": acknowledge the disappointment, focus on what you can do (refund or expedited replacement after the holiday).
- "My order is taking forever": confirm tracking, set expectations honestly.

## After Season

- Returns volume peaks in early January.
- Plan additional staffing.
- Watch for return abuse patterns (wardrobing, "free trial" returns).
