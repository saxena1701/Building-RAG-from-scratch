# Issuing Partial Refunds

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## When Partial Refunds Apply

- Restocking fee on opened electronics (15% of item price)
- Missing accessories from returned items (deduct retail cost of accessory)
- Items returned outside category window (case-by-case, manager discretion)
- Goodwill adjustments (price match retroactive, quality complaint resolution)

## Process

1. Calculate the partial refund amount with clear breakdown.
2. Get manager approval if the partial is > 30% of order value.
3. Process the partial refund through the returns portal (NOT a direct CRM adjustment).
4. Document the reason in order notes with codes:
   - RESTOCK_FEE_15
   - MISSING_ACCESSORY (and accessory description)
   - OUTSIDE_WINDOW
   - GOODWILL (and reason)

## Communication

Always explain the partial refund clearly to the customer before processing:
- The original payment amount
- The deduction amount and reason
- The expected refund amount
- The refund timing

If the customer disputes the partial:
- Listen and reconsider; sometimes new information warrants a full refund.
- If still disputing legitimately, escalate to a senior agent rather than continue to push back.
