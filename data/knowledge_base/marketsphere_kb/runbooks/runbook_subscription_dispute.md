# Subscription Auto-Renewal Disputes

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Common Scenario

Customer is charged for a subscription renewal and contests it, saying they:
- Didn't know it was a subscription
- Thought they cancelled
- Didn't get a renewal notice

## First Check

1. Confirm subscription enrollment date and method.
2. Check whether renewal notification email was sent (and to which address).
3. Check whether customer attempted to cancel (login records, support tickets).

## Resolution

**If notification was sent and customer did not act**:
- Reference the notification email.
- Explain renewal terms.
- Offer to cancel future renewals immediately.
- For first-time disputes, often appropriate to refund as goodwill (within 14 days of renewal).

**If notification was not sent or failed**:
- This is our error.
- Refund the renewal in full.
- Cancel future renewals.
- File incident report for engineering review.

**If customer claims they cancelled but it didn't go through**:
- Check audit logs for cancellation attempt.
- If logs show a failed cancellation, refund.
- If no record, give benefit of the doubt for first occurrence.

## Always Cancel Going Forward

Regardless of refund decision, cancel the subscription if the customer doesn't want it. Don't try to retain on a customer who feels they were charged unfairly.
