# Carrier Issue Escalation

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## When to Escalate Beyond Standard Trace

- Carrier trace returns "not found"
- Carrier confirms package destroyed in their facility
- High-value item lost
- Repeated carrier issues to same address
- Customer reports carrier driver behavior issues

## Carrier-Specific Escalation Paths

**UPS**: Account-level escalation through our UPS account manager. Email: ups-am@marketsphere.example.

**FedEx**: Use the priority lane in the FedEx support portal. We have a service-level agreement that includes priority response for repeat issues.

**USPS**: USPS has limited escalation; for USPS-specific issues, often more effective to file a missing-mail search and a Consumer Affairs report.

**Regional carriers (LaserShip, OnTrac)**: Lower service level overall. Tendency to mark as delivered when not. Be prepared to refund or replace more readily.

## Insurance Claims

For items > $100 lost in shipping:
1. File a carrier claim through the appropriate carrier portal.
2. Provide proof of value (invoice, packing slip).
3. Note claim number in customer's order; mention to customer that we are filing.
4. Don't make the customer wait for the claim - refund or reship immediately.
