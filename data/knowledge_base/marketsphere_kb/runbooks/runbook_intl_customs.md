# International Customs Issues

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Common Customer Issues

- Package held by customs
- Unexpected duties charged
- Long delays in clearance
- Customs return of package

## When Package is Held

1. Provide customer with the local carrier contact and the customs office (varies by country).
2. Customs often requires the recipient to provide documentation; we cannot do this for them.
3. Set expectations: clearance can take 1-3 weeks in some countries.

## Unexpected Duties

Reiterate our international policy: duties are the customer's responsibility, not MarketSphere's. We display this at checkout. If the duties seem unreasonable, suggest the customer:
- Verify with their local customs office
- Refuse the package if duties are higher than acceptable; we refund (less original shipping) when package returns to us

## Customs Return

If a package is returned by customs:
1. Refund the customer once we receive the returned package, less original international shipping.
2. Investigate why it was returned (restricted item, incorrect documentation, address issue).
3. If restricted item, do not offer to ship again; refund and note product not available for that country.
