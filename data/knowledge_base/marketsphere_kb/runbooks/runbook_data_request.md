# Customer Data Requests (Privacy)

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Types of Requests

- **Access**: Customer wants a copy of all data we hold on them.
- **Correction**: Customer wants to fix specific data.
- **Deletion**: Customer wants their account and data deleted.
- **Portability**: Customer wants their data in a portable format.

## Verification

Before processing any data request, verify identity at Level 2 (see Identity Verification runbook). For deletion requests, Level 3.

## Access Requests

1. Submit request to privacy team through internal portal.
2. Privacy team compiles report from all systems.
3. Delivered to customer within 30 days (we aim for 7 days).
4. Format is JSON + CSV, including orders, account data, support history, preferences.

## Deletion Requests

1. Confirm with customer that this is irreversible.
2. Submit to privacy team.
3. Account is deleted within 30 days.
4. Some data is retained for legal reasons:
   - Transaction records: 7 years for tax
   - Fraud-flagged accounts: indefinitely for fraud prevention
5. Notify customer when deletion is complete.

## Regional Variations

Different regions have different rights. EU (GDPR), California (CCPA), and certain other jurisdictions have stronger rights. Privacy team handles regional specifics; do not attempt to interpret regional privacy law directly.
