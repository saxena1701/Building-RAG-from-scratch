# Warranty Policy

*Last updated: 2024-08-20*

## Standard Warranty

Most MarketSphere products carry a manufacturer's warranty of one year from date of purchase, covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. Specific warranty terms vary by product and are detailed in the product manual.

## Extended Warranty (MarketSphere Care)

MarketSphere Care extends coverage to two or three years and adds accidental damage protection. Available at checkout for eligible products. See product page for eligibility and pricing.

## What's Covered

- Manufacturing defects
- Component failures under normal use
- For MarketSphere Care plans: accidental damage (drops, spills) limited to two claims per coverage period

## What's Not Covered

- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function
- Damage from misuse, neglect, or unauthorized modification
- Loss or theft
- Consumable parts (batteries beyond 6 months, filters, bulbs)

## Filing a Claim

File warranty claims through your account under Orders > Warranty Claims, or contact support with your order number and a description of the issue. Include photos where applicable.
