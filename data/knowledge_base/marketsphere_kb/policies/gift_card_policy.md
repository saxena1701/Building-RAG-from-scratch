# Gift Card Policy

*Last updated: 2024-05-22*

## Purchasing Gift Cards

Digital gift cards are available in denominations from $10 to $500. Digital cards are delivered by email within 1 hour of purchase. Physical gift cards are available in select denominations and ship as standard items.

## Redemption

Gift cards can be redeemed at checkout on MarketSphere.example. Enter the code in the "Gift Card" field. Multiple gift cards can be applied to a single order.

## Expiration

Gift cards do not expire. Balance is retained indefinitely on the card.

## Refunds

Purchases made with a gift card can be refunded to the original gift card balance. Gift cards themselves are non-refundable once purchased.

## Lost or Stolen Cards

We can replace lost or stolen digital gift cards if the customer has the original purchase confirmation. Physical gift cards cannot be replaced if lost.
