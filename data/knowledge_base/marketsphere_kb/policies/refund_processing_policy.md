# Refund Processing

*Last updated: 2024-10-18*

## How Refunds Are Issued
Refunds are issued to the original payment method whenever possible.

## Timing
- Credit card refunds: appear on statement within 5-10 business days of processing.
- PayPal: typically within 3-5 business days.
- Gift card original payments: refunded as store credit, available immediately.

## Partial Refunds
We issue partial refunds in cases of:
- Restocking fees on opened electronics (15%)
- Items missing original accessories
- Items returned outside their category window but at our discretion

## Failed Refunds
If the original card is closed or expired, contact support to arrange refund to a different payment method or store credit.
