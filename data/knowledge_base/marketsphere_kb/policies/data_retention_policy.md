# Data Retention Policy

*Last updated: 2024-02-14*

MarketSphere retains different categories of data for different durations based on legal, regulatory, and operational needs.

## Account Data
Retained for the lifetime of your account plus 90 days after closure.

## Order History
Retained for 7 years to satisfy tax and accounting regulations.

## Marketing Preferences
Retained until you unsubscribe; preference records retained for 2 years after unsubscribe.

## Customer Service Records
Calls and chats retained for 2 years for quality and training. You may request deletion subject to legal hold requirements.
