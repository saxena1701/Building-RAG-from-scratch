# Supply Chain Transparency Statement

*Last updated: 2024-03-15*

MarketSphere is committed to ethical sourcing across our supply chain.

## Vendor Standards
All vendors must agree to our Vendor Code of Conduct, which requires:
- No use of forced or child labor
- Safe working conditions
- Fair wages and reasonable hours
- Environmental compliance

## Audits
We conduct third-party audits of high-risk supplier facilities on a rotating basis.

## Reporting Concerns
Report supply chain concerns to ethics@marketsphere.example. We do not retaliate against good-faith reports.
