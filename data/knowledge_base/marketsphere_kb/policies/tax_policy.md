# Sales Tax Policy

*Last updated: 2024-04-30*

MarketSphere collects sales tax in jurisdictions where required by law. Tax rates are calculated based on the shipping destination at checkout.

## Tax-Exempt Purchases
Qualifying businesses, non-profits, and government entities can apply for tax-exempt status. Submit your tax exemption certificate through Account > Business Tools. Once approved, tax exemption applies to future orders.

## Tax on Returns
When you return an item, the sales tax originally collected is refunded along with the item price.
