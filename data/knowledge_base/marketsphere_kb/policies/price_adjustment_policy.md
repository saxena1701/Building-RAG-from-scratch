# Price Adjustment Policy

*Last updated: 2024-06-15*

If an item you purchased goes on sale within 7 days of your purchase, you can request a price adjustment for the difference.

## Eligible Adjustments
- Sale must be on MarketSphere directly.
- Item must be identical (same SKU).
- Purchase must be within 7 days of the sale price.
- Item must still be in stock at the sale price.

## How to Request
Contact support with your order number within 7 days of the sale start. The difference is refunded to the original payment method.

## Exclusions
Does not apply to flash sales under 24 hours, member-exclusive prices, or sales requiring a promo code.
