# MarketSphere Rewards Program

*Last updated: 2024-03-20*

## Earning Points

MarketSphere Rewards members earn 1 point per $1 spent on eligible purchases. Bonus point events are announced via email and in-app.

## Tiers

- **Standard**: All members. 1x points.
- **Silver**: $500+ annual spend. 1.25x points, free standard shipping.
- **Gold**: $1500+ annual spend. 1.5x points, free expedited shipping, early access to sales.
- **Platinum**: $5000+ annual spend. 2x points, free overnight shipping, dedicated support, exclusive products.

Tier status is calculated on rolling 12-month spend and updated monthly.

## Redeeming Points

100 points = $1 toward a future purchase. Points can be redeemed at checkout in increments of 500 points ($5).

## Point Expiration

Points expire 24 months after the date earned if your account has no activity. Activity includes any purchase, even a small one.

## Excluded Purchases

The following do not earn points: gift card purchases, returned items (points are reversed), purchases using employee discount, tax and shipping charges.
