# Accessibility Statement

*Last updated: 2024-05-10*

## Our Commitment

MarketSphere is committed to providing an accessible shopping experience for customers with disabilities. We strive to meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards across our digital properties.

## Website Accessibility Features

- Keyboard navigation support throughout the site
- Screen reader compatibility
- Alt text on product images
- Captioned product videos where available
- Adjustable text size
- High-contrast mode

## Customer Service Accessibility

- TTY support: 1-800-555-0199
- Email support: support@marketsphere.example
- Live chat with screen-reader-compatible interface

## Reporting Accessibility Issues

Email accessibility@marketsphere.example with details of any barriers you encounter. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
