# Promo Code Policy

*Last updated: 2024-06-10*

## How Promo Codes Work

Promo codes are applied at checkout and reduce the eligible subtotal. Only one promo code may be used per order unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Stacking Rules

Promo codes do not stack with:
- Other promo codes
- Employee discounts
- Bulk purchase discounts

Promo codes do stack with:
- Loyalty rewards
- Gift card payments
- Free shipping promotions (a free shipping code and a percentage-off code can be combined)

## Expiration

Promo codes have explicit expiration dates listed at issuance. Expired codes cannot be honored, applied retroactively, or extended.

## One Use per Customer

Most promo codes are limited to one use per customer or household. Repeated use attempts may result in order cancellation.

## Restrictions

- Codes cannot be applied to gift card purchases.
- Codes cannot be combined with price match adjustments.
- Codes do not apply to taxes or shipping unless explicitly stated.
