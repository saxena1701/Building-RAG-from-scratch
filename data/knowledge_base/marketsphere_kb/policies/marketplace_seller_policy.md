# Marketplace Seller Policy

*Last updated: 2024-08-30*

Third-party sellers on MarketSphere Marketplace must follow these requirements.

## Listing Standards
- Accurate product titles, descriptions, and images
- Honest condition ratings (new, refurbished, used)
- Real-time inventory accuracy
- Pricing transparency including all fees

## Customer Service Obligations
Sellers must respond to customer messages within 24 hours and process returns according to MarketSphere return policy timelines.

## Authentication
For designated luxury and high-counterfeit-risk categories, sellers must submit items to authentication or use only verified suppliers.
