# Hazardous Materials Shipping Policy

*Last updated: 2024-08-12*

## What Counts as Hazmat

Hazardous materials include items that are flammable, corrosive, pressurized, toxic, or otherwise regulated for transport. Common examples in our catalog:
- Lithium batteries above 100Wh
- Aerosol cans
- Certain cleaning chemicals
- Lithium-powered electronics with internal batteries above limits

## Shipping Restrictions

Hazmat items:
- Cannot be shipped by air (no overnight or 2-day to most destinations)
- Cannot be shipped internationally except by special freight arrangement
- Cannot be shipped to PO Boxes
- May require additional handling fees

## Identification

Hazmat items are clearly marked on the product page and at checkout. Estimated delivery times will reflect ground-only shipping.

## Returns

Hazmat returns require special handling. Customers cannot simply drop these items at a carrier location. Contact support for hazmat return instructions; we will arrange pickup or provide special drop-off instructions.
