# Cookie Policy

*Last updated: 2024-06-22*

## What Are Cookies
Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. MarketSphere uses cookies for essential site functionality, analytics, advertising, and personalization.

## Categories We Use
- **Essential**: Required for the site to function (cart, login).
- **Performance**: Help us understand site usage in aggregate.
- **Targeting**: Used for personalized advertising on other sites.
- **Functional**: Remember your preferences (region, language).

## Managing Cookies
Use our Cookie Preference Center at the footer of any page to opt out of non-essential cookies.
