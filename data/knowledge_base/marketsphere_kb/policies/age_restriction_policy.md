# Age-Restricted Items Policy

*Last updated: 2024-04-05*

## Items with Age Restrictions

The following categories require the purchaser to be at least 21 years of age:
- Alcohol (in jurisdictions where sale is permitted)
- Certain tobacco-adjacent products (cigar accessories, pipe accessories)

The following require age 18 or older:
- Knives over a certain blade length (varies by state)
- Air rifles and BB guns
- Certain chemical products

## Verification at Delivery

For age-restricted items, the delivery requires an adult signature with age verification. The carrier will check ID at delivery.

## Failed Delivery

If no qualified adult is available for age verification, the item is returned to our warehouse. We will refund the order in full minus the original shipping charge if the customer cannot accept on a second delivery attempt.

## Restricted Destinations

Some age-restricted items cannot be shipped to certain states or jurisdictions due to local laws. Restrictions are shown on the product page.
