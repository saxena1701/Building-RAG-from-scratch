# Damaged or Defective Items Policy

*Last updated: 2024-09-15*

## Reporting Damage or Defects

If your order arrives damaged, or an item is defective, contact support within 7 days of delivery for the fastest resolution.

## What We Need

- Order number
- Photos showing the damage or defect
- Description of the issue

## Resolution Options

For damaged or defective items, we offer:
1. **Full replacement** at no cost, with expedited shipping.
2. **Full refund** including original shipping.
3. **Partial refund** if you wish to keep the item.

You do not pay return shipping for damaged or defective items. We provide a prepaid label or arrange pickup for larger items.

## Manufacturing Defects After 30 Days

Defects that appear after the 30-day return window are handled through the warranty process. See the Warranty Policy.
