# MarketSphere Return Policy

*Last updated: 2024-11-15*

## Standard Returns

MarketSphere accepts returns on most items within **30 days** of delivery, provided the item is unused, in original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase.

## Category-Specific Windows

Different product categories have different return windows due to manufacturer agreements and product nature:

- **Electronics**: 30 days from delivery, in original sealed packaging where applicable. Opened electronics may be subject to a 15% restocking fee.
- **Apparel and footwear**: 60 days from delivery, with tags attached and unworn.
- **Home goods and furniture**: 30 days from delivery. Large items may require return shipping coordination.
- **Beauty and personal care**: 14 days from delivery, unopened only.
- **Perishables and consumables**: Not eligible for return except in case of defect.
- **Final sale items**: Not eligible for return. Items marked "Final Sale" at purchase cannot be returned or exchanged.

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log into your MarketSphere account and navigate to Orders.
2. Select the item and click "Start a Return."
3. Choose a reason and select your preferred resolution (refund or exchange).
4. Print the prepaid return label (where applicable) and ship within 7 days.

## Refund Timing

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receipt at our returns center. The funds typically post to the original payment method within an additional 3-5 business days depending on the issuer.

## Exceptions

Items damaged in transit, defective on arrival, or shipped incorrectly are eligible for return regardless of category-specific windows and do not incur restocking fees. See the "Damaged or Defective Items" policy for details.
