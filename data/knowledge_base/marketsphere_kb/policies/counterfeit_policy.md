# Counterfeit and Authenticity Policy

*Last updated: 2024-06-30*

## Our Commitment

MarketSphere sells only authentic products. We source from authorized distributors, brand partners, and verified manufacturers. We do not knowingly list or sell counterfeit goods.

## What to Do if You Suspect a Counterfeit

If you receive an item you believe is counterfeit:
1. Do not use the item.
2. Contact support within 7 days of delivery with photos.
3. We will issue a prepaid return label and full refund upon receipt.
4. We will investigate the listing and supplier.

## Marketplace Sellers

Items sold by third-party sellers through MarketSphere Marketplace are subject to seller authentication requirements. We do periodic audits and remove sellers found to violate authenticity standards.

## Reporting

To report suspected counterfeit listings, email counterfeit@marketsphere.example with the product URL and details.
