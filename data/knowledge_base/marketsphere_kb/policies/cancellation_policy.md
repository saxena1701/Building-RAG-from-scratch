# Order Cancellation Policy

*Last updated: 2024-08-01*

## When You Can Cancel

You can cancel an order at no charge if it has not yet shipped. Most orders enter shipment processing within 1-2 business days of order placement.

## How to Cancel

- Log into your account, go to Orders, and click "Cancel Order" on the eligible order.
- Or contact support with your order number.

## After Shipment

Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the shipment at delivery or initiate a return upon receipt. Refused shipments will be refunded once received at our returns center, less return shipping if applicable.

## Partial Cancellation

You can cancel individual line items on multi-item orders if they have not entered shipment processing. Already-shipped items must be returned through the standard return process.

## Subscription Cancellation

See the Subscription Policy for cancelling recurring subscriptions.
