# Subscription Policy

*Last updated: 2024-04-18*

## Subscription Products

Subscription products ship on a recurring schedule (monthly, quarterly, or annually) at a discounted rate. You manage subscriptions through Account > Subscriptions.

## Modifying Subscriptions

You can change frequency, skip a delivery, or update payment method at any time before the next billing date. Changes take effect on the next cycle.

## Cancelling

Cancel anytime through Account > Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect immediately; no future charges will be made. You will receive any shipments already in processing.

## Failed Payments

If a subscription payment fails, we will retry the payment three times over 7 days. If all attempts fail, the subscription is paused until you update payment information.

## Subscription Discounts

Subscriptions receive a standard 10% discount off the one-time purchase price. Some products offer 15% discount for annual frequency.
