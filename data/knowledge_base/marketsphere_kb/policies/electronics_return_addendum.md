# Electronics Return Policy Addendum

*Last updated: 2024-11-15*

## Electronics Returns - Detailed Policy

This addendum to the main return policy provides additional detail for electronics.

### Return Window

Electronics may be returned within **30 days** of delivery date, consistent with our standard policy.

### Opened Items

Opened electronics are accepted within the 30-day window but are subject to a **15% restocking fee** unless:
- The item is defective
- The item was damaged in shipping
- The item was not as described

### Excluded Items

The following electronics categories are non-returnable once opened:
- Software, digital downloads, and digital subscriptions
- Items with broken manufacturer seals on hygiene-sensitive components (earbuds, in-ear thermometers)
- Custom-configured items (PCs built to order, engraved products)

### Manufacturer Warranties

Many electronics carry separate manufacturer warranties that may extend beyond our return window. Review the product manual for warranty information.
