# Auto-Renewal Policy

*Last updated: 2024-07-19*

Subscriptions and memberships marked as auto-renewing will renew automatically at the end of each term. You are notified 7 days before renewal with the renewal amount and date. Cancel anytime before renewal through your account.

## Refund After Renewal

If you forget to cancel and are charged for a new term, you have 14 days from the renewal charge to request a refund, provided you have not used the renewal-period benefits.
