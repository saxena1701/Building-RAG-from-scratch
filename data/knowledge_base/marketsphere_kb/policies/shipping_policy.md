# MarketSphere Shipping Policy

*Last updated: 2024-10-01*

## Shipping Methods and Timing

MarketSphere offers multiple shipping options on every order. Estimated delivery times begin from the date of shipment, not the date of order.

### Standard Shipping (Free over $50)

- Delivery in 3-5 business days within the continental United States.
- **Free on orders over $50** after discounts. Orders under $50 incur a $5.95 shipping fee.
- Available to all 50 states.

### Expedited Shipping

- 2 business days, $12.95 flat rate.
- Order by 2pm local time for same-day processing.

### Overnight Shipping

- Next business day delivery, $24.95 flat rate.
- Order by noon local time for same-day processing. Not available to Alaska, Hawaii, or PO boxes.

### International Shipping

- Available to 40+ countries. See the International Shipping Policy for the full list, rates, and delivery times.
- Customs duties and import taxes are the customer's responsibility.

## Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. You will receive a shipment confirmation email with tracking information once your order ships.

## Address Changes

Address changes are possible only before the order has shipped. Contact support immediately if you need to update an address. Once shipped, address changes must be coordinated with the carrier.

## Shipping to PO Boxes and APO/FPO

- Standard shipping is available to PO Boxes and APO/FPO addresses.
- Expedited and overnight shipping are not available to these addresses.
