# International Shipping Policy

*Last updated: 2024-10-01*

## Where We Ship

MarketSphere ships internationally to 42 countries across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East. The current list is maintained at marketsphere.example/international.

## Shipping Costs

International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination, weight, and dimensions. Costs typically range from $15 to $85 for standard international.

## Delivery Times

- Canada, Mexico: 5-10 business days
- Europe: 7-14 business days
- Asia-Pacific: 10-20 business days
- Middle East: 10-15 business days

## Duties and Taxes

The customer is responsible for all import duties, taxes, and customs fees. These are not included in the shipping cost shown at checkout. Charges vary by country.

## International Returns

International returns require customer-paid return shipping unless the item is damaged or defective. Refunds exclude original international shipping charges.

## Restricted Items

Some products cannot be shipped internationally due to regulations:
- Lithium batteries above certain capacities
- Aerosols and pressurized containers
- Certain electronics with regional restrictions
- Hazmat-classified items
