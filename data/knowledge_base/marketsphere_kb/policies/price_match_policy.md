# Price Match Policy

*Last updated: 2024-07-15*

## Our Price Match Guarantee

If you find a lower price on an identical item at a qualifying competitor within 14 days of your purchase, we'll match the difference.

## Eligible Competitors

We match prices from these retailers when sold and shipped by them directly:
- Amazon (sold by Amazon, not third-party sellers)
- Walmart (online and in-store, not Marketplace)
- Target (online and in-store)
- Best Buy
- Costco (for members)

## Eligibility Requirements

- The item must be identical: same brand, model number, color, size, and condition.
- The competitor's item must be in stock at the lower price.
- The lower price must be on the competitor's primary domain (not third-party sellers).
- Limited-time flash sales, doorbusters, and member-only prices are not eligible.

## How to Claim

Within 14 days of purchase, contact support with your MarketSphere order number and a link or screenshot of the competitor's listing showing the lower price.

## Exclusions

Bundle deals, clearance items, refurbished items, and items purchased with promo codes greater than 15% off are not eligible for price match.
