# Accepted Payment Methods

*Last updated: 2024-07-01*

## Supported Payment Types

MarketSphere accepts:
- Major credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- Debit cards with Visa or Mastercard logos
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- MarketSphere gift cards and store credit
- Buy Now, Pay Later: Affirm and Klarna (eligibility based on order total)
- Wire transfer (for orders over $1000, B2B accounts only)

## Authorization

When you place an order, we place a temporary authorization hold on your payment method for the order total. The hold is released and replaced with the actual charge when the order ships.

## Multi-Step Payments

You can split payment across multiple methods at checkout - for example, applying a gift card and paying the remainder with a credit card.

## International Payments

International orders can use major credit cards and PayPal. Some local payment methods are supported in specific countries; see the international checkout page for details.

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails, you will be prompted to update your payment method. Orders are not held; if payment is not provided within 24 hours, the order is cancelled.
