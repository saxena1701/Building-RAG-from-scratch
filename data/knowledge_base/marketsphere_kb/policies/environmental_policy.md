# Environmental Policy

*Last updated: 2024-02-28*

MarketSphere works to reduce the environmental impact of our operations.

## Packaging
- Curbside-recyclable packaging on 85% of orders
- Right-sized boxes to reduce material and shipping emissions
- Recycled content in shipping cartons (minimum 40%)

## Carbon Neutral Shipping
We offer carbon-neutral shipping on all orders at no extra cost, through partnerships with verified carbon offset programs.

## Returns Sustainability
Returns are inspected and routed to resale, donation, or recycling channels rather than landfill where possible.
