# Shipping Policy (Archived 2023)

*Last updated: 2023-01-10*

## MarketSphere Shipping Policy

*This document is the 2023 archived version of our shipping policy. It is retained for historical reference and contract dispute resolution. For current shipping information, refer to the latest shipping policy.*

### Shipping Methods

- **Standard shipping**: Free on orders over **$35**, otherwise $4.95. Delivery in 4-6 business days.
- **Expedited shipping**: $9.95, 3 business days.
- **Overnight shipping**: $19.95, next business day.

### International Shipping

International shipping was available to 25 countries in 2023, with a flat rate of $24.95 for standard international delivery.

### Address Changes

Address changes were processed manually by our support team. Contact support within 12 hours of order placement.
