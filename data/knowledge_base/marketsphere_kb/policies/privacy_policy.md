# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2024-09-01*

## Information We Collect

MarketSphere collects information you provide directly (name, email, shipping address, payment information), information about your transactions, and information collected automatically (device, browser, IP address, browsing behavior).

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to process orders, communicate about your account, personalize your experience, prevent fraud, and improve our services. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## Sharing

We share information with service providers (payment processors, shipping carriers, fraud detection), legal authorities when required, and business partners in the case of merger or acquisition.

## Your Rights

Depending on jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. Submit requests through Account > Privacy or contact privacy@marketsphere.example.

## Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. Transaction records are retained for seven years for tax and regulatory purposes.

## Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for details on what cookies we use and how to manage them.
