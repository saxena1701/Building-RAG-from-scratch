# How do I know if something I bought was recalled?

*Last updated: 2024-09-01*

## Question

How do I know if something I bought was recalled?

## Answer

If a product you purchased is recalled, we send an email to the order email address. You can also check our Recalls page at marketsphere.example/recalls. For urgent safety recalls, follow the instructions in the recall notice; we typically offer full refunds and free return shipping.
