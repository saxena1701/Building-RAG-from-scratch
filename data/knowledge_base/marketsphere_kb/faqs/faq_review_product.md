# How do I review a product I bought?

*Last updated: 2024-09-01*

## Question

How do I review a product I bought?

## Answer

After your order is delivered, you'll receive an email inviting you to review the products. You can also log into your account and go to Orders > Write a Review. Reviews must follow our community guidelines (no profanity, no personal information, on-topic to the product).
