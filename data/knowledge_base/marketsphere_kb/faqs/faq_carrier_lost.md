# The carrier says delivered but I don't have it. What do I do?

*Last updated: 2024-09-01*

## Question

The carrier says delivered but I don't have it. What do I do?

## Answer

Check around your delivery location, with neighbors, and your building manager (for apartments). Sometimes packages are scanned as delivered slightly before arrival. If still missing after 48 hours, contact support and we'll initiate a carrier claim and either reship or refund.
