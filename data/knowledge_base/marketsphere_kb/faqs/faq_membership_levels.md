# What are the rewards tiers?

*Last updated: 2024-09-01*

## Question

What are the rewards tiers?

## Answer

MarketSphere Rewards has four tiers based on annual spend: Standard (all members), Silver ($500+), Gold ($1500+), and Platinum ($5000+). Higher tiers earn more points per dollar and unlock shipping perks. Tier status is recalculated monthly based on rolling 12-month spend.
