# An item is missing from my order. What do I do?

*Last updated: 2024-09-01*

## Question

An item is missing from my order. What do I do?

## Answer

First check if your order shipped in multiple boxes (shipment confirmation emails show how many shipments are in the order). If you're still missing an item, contact support within 7 days of delivery with your order number, and we'll investigate and issue a refund or replacement.
