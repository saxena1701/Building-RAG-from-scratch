# When will I get my refund?

*Last updated: 2024-09-01*

## Question

When will I get my refund?

## Answer

Once your return arrives at our returns center, we process the refund within 5-7 business days. After we issue the refund, your bank or card issuer typically posts the funds in 3-5 additional business days. Total time from drop-off to funds available is usually 10-12 business days.
