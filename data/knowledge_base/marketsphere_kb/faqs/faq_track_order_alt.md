# Where is my order? How do I see shipping status?

*Last updated: 2024-09-01*

## Question

Where is my order? How do I see shipping status?

## Answer

After your order ships, look for a shipment confirmation email from MarketSphere with your tracking number. The tracking link in that email shows real-time carrier updates. You can also access tracking via Account > Orders > [your order] > View Tracking.
